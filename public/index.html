✸ Rebirth

In Old Eden, death is not the end — it is the mechanism of change.

When you die, your character's consciousness is shattered into Soul Shards that scatter across the galaxy. Other players can absorb these shards to gain fragments of your skills, memories, and genetic traits.

You are then reborn into an NPC — inheriting their life, their genome, their place in the universe. Your new identity is determined by the Rebirth Lottery.
